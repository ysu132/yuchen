This paper proposes a psychology-inspired Humour Decomposition Mechanism (HDM) that uses chain-of-thought reasoning to imitate aspects of the human thought process and improve the readability of translated humorous text. By incorporating humour theory, HDM further strengthens humorous elements in translation. Experiments on open-source humour datasets report average improvements of 7.75% in humour, 2.81% in fluency, and 6.13% in coherence.
