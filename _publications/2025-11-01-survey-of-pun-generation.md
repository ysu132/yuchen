Pun generation creatively modifies linguistic elements to produce humour or evoke double meanings while preserving coherence and contextual appropriateness. This survey reviews pun-generation datasets and methods, including traditional approaches, deep learning techniques, and pre-trained language models. It also summarises automated and human evaluation metrics, discusses research challenges, and outlines promising directions for future work.
