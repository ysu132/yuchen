This paper presents a three-layer framework for adaptive and auditable policy interventions in agent-based simulations. LLMs serve as diagnostic instruments that produce structured risk assessments, while deterministic formulas translate those assessments into traceable policy updates. Experiments in an elderly-care simulation show that explicit control rules can improve performance while preserving auditability.
