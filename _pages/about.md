I'm a third-year Ph.D. student in the [School of Computer Science](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/science/about-the-faculty/school-of-computer-science.html) at the [University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/), New Zealand.

My research interests include natural language processing, multimodal reasoning, computational humour, pun understanding and generation, knowledge engineering, and AI agents. I am fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Michael Witbrock](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/m-witbrock) in the Strong AI Lab (SAIL), University of Auckland and [Prof. Diana Benavides-Prado](https://dianabenavidesprado.github.io/).
