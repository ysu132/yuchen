I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in the [School of Computer Science](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/science/about-the-faculty/school-of-computer-science.html) at the [University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/), New Zealand.

My research interests include natural language processing, multimodal large language models, computational humour, pun understanding and generation, knowledge engineering, and AI agents.

I am fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Michael Witbrock](https://profiles.auckland.ac.nz/m-witbrock) in the Strong AI Lab (SAIL), University of Auckland. I also work closely with Dr. Diana Benavides Prado on research in computational humour and multimodal reasoning.

My recent research focuses on computational humour, especially text and speech pun understanding, benchmark construction, reasoning with large language models, and knowledge-enhanced language models. My work has been published at conferences including EMNLP and ICONIP.
